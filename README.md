# JIGGLE KINGS

Up to four translucent gelatin kings on a toy island, holding out for fifteen minutes against a growing horde of candy
creatures. Your toys fire on their own (flails, bowling balls, yo-yos, soda rockets, bubble wands, wind-up chompers and
more), and your own wobbly body is a weapon too: slam into the crowd, belly-bump it, roll through it, shove beach balls
and barrels into it. Pick an upgrade every level, evolve maxed toys, revive your friends, and beat the three bosses.

**Play it** at https://gamesbyai.github.io/jiggle-kings/. Quick Play drops you into an open party, Create Party gives you
a code and an invite link for friends, and Solo runs entirely in your browser. It's made for desktop browsers.

## Controls

| | Keyboard / mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD | left stick |
| Look | mouse | right stick |
| Jump (hold for a super jump) | Space | A |
| Slam (in the air) / belly bump (on the ground) | Ctrl, left click or Q | B |
| Roll | Shift, right click or E | X |
| Pick a level-up card | 1 – 4 (R rerolls) | |
| Menu | Esc | Start |

## What's in it

- 12 toy weapons, each with an evolution (max the toy and own its paired passive), and 12 passives
- 7 kinds of candy enemies, crowned elites, horde surges and 3 bosses at 5, 10 and 15 minutes
- Co-op for up to 4: one shared level, your own build, revive downed friends by standing next to them
- Real physics everywhere: enemies get launched, take fall damage and drown in the sea

This repository holds the built game that GitHub Pages serves; the multiplayer game server runs separately. It was made
with Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code.
